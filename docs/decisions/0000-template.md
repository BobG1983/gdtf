# NNNN. [Short decision title]

> Copy this file to `NNNN-kebab-slug.md` (next free number), fill in every
> section, then open it `Proposed`. Flip to `Accepted` once the decision is
> made; never edit an Accepted ADR — supersede it with a new one. See
> [index.md](index.md) for the rules.

## Status

`Proposed` — one of `Proposed` / `Accepted` / `Superseded by NNNN`.
Date and the Linear ticket (`GTW-N`) that drove the decision, if any.

## Context

The forces at play: the problem, the constraints, the design canon
(`docs/pillars/`, `docs/combat/`, `docs/glossary.md`) and any tickets that
bear on it. State the situation factually — enough that a future reader
understands *why a decision was even needed*, without already knowing the
outcome.

## Decision

The decision, stated plainly in active voice ("We will …"). Be specific and
unambiguous: a reader should be able to tell whether a given change conforms to
this ADR or violates it.

## Consequences

What becomes true once this is adopted — the good, the bad, and the neutral.
Include new constraints created, work it enables or forecloses, and anything a
future ADR would have to revisit to undo it.

## Alternatives considered

Each option that was genuinely on the table, and why it was *not* chosen.
"Do nothing" / "keep the status quo" is a legitimate alternative — record it
when it applied.
