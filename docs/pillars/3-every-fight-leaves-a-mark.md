# Pillar 3: Every Fight Leaves a Mark

**Consequences are permanent and they compound — a won fight can still cost you.**

The core loop is *fight → consequences on the survivors → carry the scarred roster forward → fight again, changed.* This is the engine of fun; everything else is a layer on top of it. Build and prove it first.

Permadeath and lasting Injuries mean no fight is free. A wound rolls at the moment it is taken and persists into the campaign even if the ganger finishes the battle standing — a triumphant victory can still leave your best shooter half-blind. The roster you bring to the next fight is exactly the roster the last fight left you: down a leader, up a grudge, carrying injuries that change how each survivor plays.

Because the marks are permanent, they accumulate into a trajectory — a gang's history is written in its scars. Systems that hand out costless do-overs, full heals between every fight, or disposable gangers undercut this pillar. The weight of the loop comes from the fact that it *remembers*.
