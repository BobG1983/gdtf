# Pillar 4: Permanent Stakes Make Decisions Matter

**Tension comes from consequence, not a clock — every push, hold, and risk carries lasting weight.**

This is a turn-based tactics game; it does not manufacture pressure with a countdown timer. The pressure is *the stakes*. Because loss is permanent — death, lasting injury, capture — every tactical choice is a real bet with real downside.

Do you push your wounded veteran one more tile for the kill, or pull him out while he can still walk? Do you sprint across open ground to reach melee — eating reaction fire on the approach — to land a decisive blow? Do you spend your Time Units now or hoard them to stay dangerous on the enemy turn? Each of these is a genuine decision *because* getting it wrong costs something you can't get back.

This is the litmus for combat mechanics: a mechanic must create a **decision**, not execute automatically. If there's no meaningful choice and no real downside, it's a stat, not a mechanic. The risk *is* the texture — melee's power, for example, is balanced by the danger of closing the distance, not by capping its damage.
