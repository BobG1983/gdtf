# Pillar 5: Readability Over Fidelity

**We compete on legible consequence, not production values.**

We are not going to out-render a AAA studio, and we shouldn't try (Rohrer's whole point). The presentation is UFO:Enemy Unknown 2D tactical grid, abstract readable units, and — above all — **text-forward after-action reports**.

The grimdark lives in a well-typeset consequence log, not a sprite budget. *"Krieg lost his eye and swears he'll kill the Goliath who did it"* carries more weight than any animation we could afford. The reading experience after a fight — who fell, who held, who now hates whom — is a first-class part of the game, not an afterthought. Proof points: Into the Breach (total board readability) and Dwarf Fortress (stories from pure systems + text).

Practically: spend the budget on the systems that generate stories, keep every game state instantly legible, and never trade clarity for visual flourish. If the player can't read the situation at a glance, the situation can't generate a decision.
