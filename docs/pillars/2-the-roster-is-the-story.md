# Pillar 2: The Roster Is the Story

**Gangers earn their identity from wounds, XP, and grudges — never from a class or a matchup slot.**

A one-eyed, limping veteran with a vendetta against the Goliath who took his eye is the product the game sells. Identity is *accrued through play*, not assigned at character creation. Two gangers who start identical diverge into distinct people over a campaign because of what happened to them — the scars, the kills, the near-deaths, the rivals.

This is *why* matchups live on gear rather than on unit types (see [Pillar 6](6-matchups-are-modifiers.md)). A unit-type counter wheel would force seven archetypes and collapse named gangers into "the rock guy" — destroying the very thing the roster is for. Mechanics that flatten gangers into interchangeable stat blocks work against this pillar.

Protect the layer that turns combatants into people: the wound table, the advancement track, the grudge ledger. That layer is the moat (see [Pillar 8](8-grudges-with-memory.md)) and the reason anyone remembers a run. See [../combat/wounds-and-roster.md](../combat/wounds-and-roster.md).
