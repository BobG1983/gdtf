# Pillar 6: Matchups Are Modifiers, Never Auto-Wins

**Counters are felt but never decisive — never a pre-mission loadout puzzle.**

The 7-type weapon/armor wheel lives on *gear*, not on unit types, and it modifies **weapon punch & shred only** — a scale-independent, asymmetric multiplier. A favorable matchup makes a hit bite harder; a resisted one makes it skip off. It never bypasses armor and never decides a fight on its own.

If matchups auto-won, the entire tactical layer would collapse into a pre-mission question — "did I bring the right gun? yes/no" — and the battle would be decided before it started. That is the failure mode this pillar exists to prevent. The counter must be one input among many: cover, positioning, Time Units, wounds, skill.

Keeping matchups as a *modifier* is also what lets them sit on gear instead of on gangers, which protects [Pillar 2](2-the-roster-is-the-story.md) — a veteran can re-arm to counter the gang he has a grudge against, and *that re-arming is a decision*, not a destiny.
